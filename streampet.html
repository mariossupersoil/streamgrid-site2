Questions

Good to know.

What can I spread on it?

Plain yogurt, wet food or peanut butter. Choose peanut butter without xylitol, which is toxic to dogs.

Is it safe for my pet?

It is made from food-grade silicone, with thick, rounded shapes and no thin spikes. Always supervise your pet, and take it away if they start chewing it.

How do I clean it?

Rinse it right after use, then wash it in warm, soapy water. A small brush gets into the cells.
