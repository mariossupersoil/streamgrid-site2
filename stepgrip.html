Questions

Good to know.

Will it stick to my tub?

StepGrip grips smooth, clean surfaces such as acrylic, enamel and glazed tile. It may not grip textured anti-slip tub floors.

Is it safe to stand on?

Place it on a clean, smooth floor and press it down firmly before stepping on. Use a grab bar if you have one, and take care on any wet floor.

How do I clean it?

Rinse it after each shower and hang it by the slot to dry. Wash it in warm, soapy water once a week.
