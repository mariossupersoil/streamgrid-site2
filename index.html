Questions

Good to know.

Will it fit my toilet?

StreamGrid is designed for standard household bowls. Its flex grooves let it bend to the curve, and the strap adjusts the height in small steps.

How do I clean it?

Lift it out once a week and rinse it in warm, soapy water, then hang it back up. It is one piece, so there is nothing to take apart.

Does the clip mark the porcelain?

The clip attaches with waterproof foam tape, not screws or glue. It sits just under the rim, out of sight.

How often should I replace it?

Every 3 months, for the freshest bathroom. Your clip stays in place, so you only need a refill mat.

How does the subscription work?

You pay today and receive your set with its clip. Every 3 months after that, we send fresh mats and charge the subscription price. In Canada it always ships free, and you can cancel anytime from the "Manage subscription" link at the bottom of this page.
